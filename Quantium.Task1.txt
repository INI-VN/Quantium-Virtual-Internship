##Lets us t-test to clearly see the differences

###t-test
comp_data$AVG_PRICE <- comp_data$TOT_SALES / comp_data$PROD_QTY

#### mainstream vs premium
t.test(AVG_PRICE ~ PREMIUM_CUSTOMER, data = comp_data %>%
         filter(PREMIUM_CUSTOMER %in% c("Mainstream", "Premium")))
#####t = 11.057, df = 137767, p-value < 2.2e-16
#####there is significance difference in the average unit price of mainstream and premium...with mainstream having higher mean

#### mainstream vs budget
t.test(AVG_PRICE ~ PREMIUM_CUSTOMER, data = comp_data %>%
         filter(PREMIUM_CUSTOMER %in% c("Mainstream", "Budget")))
#####t = -14.241, df = 179589, p-value < 2.2e-16
#####there is significance difference in the average unit price of mainstream and budget...with mainstream having higher mean
